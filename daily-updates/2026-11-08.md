## Actionable

`agent-autoloop` describes exactly what our cron jobs are missing: a
**decision card** instead of a silent failure. When a scheduled run hits an
ambiguous state it currently either does the wrong thing or dies. The cheap
version for us: any job that would reply `AUTOMATION_FAILED` should carry the
one question a human could answer in ten seconds, not just the stack. Patch
the three highest-frequency cron prompts, not all of them.
